Border is the other name for prejudice.

NBO is a call to play across the borders of purported, usual, and to listen without prejudices.
NBO is a tool and a symbol to help overcome a violent history, to set an example on how people can work and live together.
No Borders Orchestra is about making a decision that art should represent the change that we wish to see in the world.
